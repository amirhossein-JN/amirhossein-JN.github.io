About Me

❤️ Who I Am

Hello! I'm Amirhossein, a student of Computer Networks & Software Engineering at Allameh Helli 7 (SAMPAD).

I'm passionate about software development, artificial intelligence, robotics, computer vision, algorithms, and building practical systems that solve real-world problems.

My competitive robotics experience has also given me the opportunity to work on autonomous systems, navigation, mapping, sensor processing, path planning, obstacle detection, and computer vision.

🏆 Competition achievements: I achieved 1st place at the national level in the RoboCupJunior Rescue Simulation League with the Nova team. I also achieved 3rd place in the RoboCup Soccer Simulation 3D League as a member of Binary Union.

I'm continuously improving my skills through competitive projects, software development, and hands-on learning, with a growing focus on AI and intelligent robotics.
